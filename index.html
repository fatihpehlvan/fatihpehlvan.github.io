Research

I’m interested in game agents that play with people rather than only against them: teammates that coordinate, call things out and can explain what they are doing. That means multi-agent reinforcement learning, human–AI teaming in games, explainable RL and 3D characters.

My master’s work is on 3D human avatars. Before that I worked on how well large language models spot design problems in code.
