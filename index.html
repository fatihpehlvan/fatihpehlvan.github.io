Fatih Pehlivan

I’m a computer engineering master’s student at Bilkent University and a research assistant at Hacettepe University in Ankara. I’m interested in game agents that play with people rather than only against them: teammates that coordinate, call things out and can explain what they are doing.

Research

Multi-agent reinforcement learning, human–AI teaming in games, explainable RL and 3D characters. My master’s work is on 3D human avatars. Before that I worked on how well large language models spot design problems in code.

Projects

  1. 2026

    Battle commander for Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord

    With M. Y. Bilge. A game mod in which an agent commands formations instead of single soldiers: advance, fall back, flank. The environment is built and reinforcement learning runs start in December. We want to see how much command quality alone changes a battle.

    Dev log (in Turkish)

  2. 2025

    Personality in motion

    With A. Ü. Ergüzen. Unity ML-Agents characters trained with PPO and curriculum learning to move in a way that reads as extraverted, with rewards built on Laban movement qualities. We tested the results in an online user study.

  3. 2024

    Gunslinger

    B.Sc. graduation project, team of three. A 3D multiplayer version of the card game BANG! in Unity. AI players take over when someone disconnects, so the match goes on.
